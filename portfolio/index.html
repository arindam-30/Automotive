About Me

I'm a Masters student in Automotive Engineering & Motorsports at Politecnico di Milano, specialising in vehicle dynamics and dynamic system simulation. Over four years with Pravega Racing's Formula Student team, I've worked across suspension kinematics, drivetrain design, vehicle setup, and on-track testing, correlating simulation against real test data at Formula Bharat, FS East, and FS Germany.

I use MATLAB/Simulink and Python to build longitudinal, lateral, ride, and multibody vehicle models, with a focus on translating simulation and telemetry into concrete performance improvements. I also use LLM-assisted development to accelerate technical workflows, debugging, and exploration of simulation methodologies. As former Team Captain, I led a 30-member engineering team through vehicle development and competition.

Python TensorFlow Data Science

FSAE EV Powertrain Design

MATLAB/Simulink Powertrain Thermal

Simscape Multibody Ongoing
